murmur — tape-deck prototype

Sizes for review: deck height 50px, disc 36px, timer + speed 13px in Departure Mono on the pixel grid (with VT323 as fallback if Departure Mono isn't installed locally — drop DepartureMono.woff2 in this folder to see the real thing). Mark is Fraunces Italic m at 11px weight 300, upright, off-center to the right of disc center. Disc rotates one revolution per 3s while playing. The "mark behavior" comparison row at the bottom shows the two options: mark rotating with the disc (going upside-down half the time) versus mark counter-rotating to stay upright.

The mark goes around with the disc. It's right-side-up half the time and upside-down the other half. Mechanically truthful: a real knob's indicator does this. The mark reads as ornament rather than as a letter, which is the design intent.

The mark orbits around the disc center but counter-rotates to stay upright — always reads as m. Less mechanically truthful (a real indicator pivots with the dial) but more readable. Picks up a "compass needle" feel.
